## 1. Overview: Fresh Produce, Delivered at Scale

Nairat Food Services is a Doha-based importer and distributor of fresh fruits and vegetables. Founded in 2020, we have grown into one of the main produce suppliers to Qatar's hypermarket and supermarket chains, and today serve retail, wholesale, foodservice, and online grocery customers across the country.

We source from more than 15 origin countries and from farms here in Qatar, bring every shipment through our own HACCP and ISO 22000:2018 certified facility, and deliver it in our own refrigerated fleet. Customers get one accountable partner from farm to shelf, with a wide range, consistent quality, and the daily reliability that fresh categories demand.

## 3. Corporate Vision & Mission

### Vision
> *"To be Qatar's premier supplier of fresh fruits and vegetables: the partner retailers rely on for range, quality and availability in every season."*

### Mission
> *"To supply high-quality, affordable fresh produce with food safety built into every step, to keep our people safe and growing, and to be a long-term, trusted partner to our customers, suppliers and community."*

### Core Values (What We Stand For)
1. **Safety First**: Food safety and workplace safety are non-negotiable. Certified systems, trained people, and checks that happen every day, not once a year.
2. **Fresh, Every Day**: Short lead times and an unbroken cold chain from the moment a shipment lands to the moment it reaches the shelf.
3. **Reliable at Scale**: Volumes and planning that keep more than 100 branches stocked, with availability mapped across origins and seasons.
4. **True Partnership**: We work as an extension of our customers' fresh teams, from forecasting and pricing to full category management.

## 4. What We Do (Four Operating Channels & Core Capabilities)

1. **Global Sourcing and Import**: Direct relationships with growers and exporters across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia. More than 1,200 active lines selected for quality, shelf life, and value, consolidated through our own import operation.
2. **Retail Supply and Category Management**: Daily supply to hypermarket and supermarket chains, from basic replenishment to full category management of the fresh section, including range planning, pricing, and in-store availability.
3. **Wholesale and Foodservice**: Bulk supply to wholesalers, caterers, restaurants, and independent grocers, with the same sorting, packing, and cold-chain standards as our retail programme.
4. **Quick-Commerce and Online Grocery**: Cross-dock supply to dark-store and online grocery networks, packed to order and delivered to their operating rhythm, with weekly price lists and short lead times.
5. **Cold Storage and Logistics for Partners**: Temperature-controlled cold rooms with pallet racking, forklift handling, order picking, and refrigerated delivery for retail and e-commerce partners needing a central warehouse in Doha.
6. **Local Farm Partnerships**: Through our agreement with Mahaseel, Qatar's agricultural marketing company, we bring Qatari-grown vegetables to market alongside imports, supporting local farmers and national food security.

## 6. Food Safety & Accreditations

- **HACCP Certificate of Compliance**: Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points, Codex Alimentarius.
- **ISO 22000:2018 Certificate of Registration**: Food Safety Management System.
  - *Certified Scope*: Receiving, storage, sorting, packing, and dispatch of fresh fruits and vegetables; receiving, storage, and dispatch of dry fruits and nuts.
- **Dedicated Food Safety & Hygiene Team**: Full-time department enforcing continuous employee training and audits.
- **5-Step Control Process**:
  1. *Receiving*: Temperature and quality checks on arrival; rejection of non-conforming goods.
  2. *Sorting & Grading*: Inspected and graded in hygiene-controlled hall.
  3. *Packing*: Packed to customer specifications with full lot traceability.
  4. *Cold Storage*: Monitored 24/7 at target temperature.
  5. *Dispatch & Delivery*: Fleet of 40 refrigerated vehicles ensuring unbroken cold chain.
